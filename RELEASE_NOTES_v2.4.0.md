# GeneWise v2.4.0 — Clinical-Grade Genetics Literacy & Multimodal Intelligence Platform

**GeneWise v2.4.0** marks the enterprise release of the platform, combining evidence-grounded clinical decision support, interactive 3D molecular genomics, longitudinal Bi-LSTM trajectory forecasting, and multi-sensor ICU telemetry.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Major Features

### 1. 🧬 Genomics Academy & 10 Virtual Labs
- **Full Curriculum**: 12 structured medical courses (GEN-101 through GEN-112) aligned with ACMG and NSGC core competencies.
- **Interactive 3D Molecular Simulation**: Real-time canvas WebGL DNA Double-Helix viewer with Watson-Crick base-pairing topology, major/minor groove annotations, nucleotide inspection, and speed controls.
- **Dynamic Certificate Registry**: Cryptographically hashed completion credentials with single-page A4 landscape print-to-PDF formatting and verifiable QR signatures.

### 2. 🔬 Genes & Genetics Explorer (`/genetics` & `/diseases`)
- **ClinVar & gnomAD Provenance**: Variant classification (Pathogenic, Benign, VUS) covering 1,428 curated entries and 2,109 active loci.
- **3D Cryo-EM & PDB Protein Mapping**: Structural models and exon-domain architectures.
- **Disease Explorer**: Catalog with multi-inheritance filters (Autosomal Dominant, Autosomal Recessive, X-Linked).

### 3. 💓 Real-Time ICU Telemetry Node #409
- **Multi-Lead Vectors**: Interactive streaming for Lead-II ECG, Precordial V1 vector, SpO2 Optical Plethysmogram, and Thoracic Pneumography.
- **ACLS Arrhythmia Presets**: Normal Sinus (72 bpm), Sinus Tachycardia (128 bpm), Sinus Bradycardia (44 bpm), and Atrial Fibrillation (114 bpm).
- **Oscilloscope Inspection**: Stream freeze/resume controls, acute cardiac spike injection, and one-click clinical telemetry strip export.

### 4. 🤖 Multilingual AI Clinical Assistant & Voice Engine
- **Duplex Voice Telemetry**: Speech-to-speech interaction across 13 Indian languages with barge-in interruption handling.
- **Predictive AI Engines**: Bi-LSTM temporal trajectory forecasting (ROC-AUC 0.958), Celiac ML classifier (ROC-AUC 0.962), and TreeSHAP attribution studio.

### 5. 🛡️ Enterprise Security Architecture v2.0
- **ReBAC & TOTP MFA**: RFC 6238 two-factor authentication and active physician-patient care relationship checks.
- **Immutable Audit Logging**: Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature chains conforming to HIPAA § 164.312 and DPDP Act 2023.
